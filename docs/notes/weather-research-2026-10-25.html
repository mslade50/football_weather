Weather moves scoring. A betting edge needs a stronger test.

No new profitable betting strategy is established by this research. The best candidate for a prospective test is buying an under when a public wind forecast first crosses a fixed threshold, at an actual book’s paired line and odds. Stadium multipliers, a benign-weather overs process, and automatic rain, snow or temperature bets do not clear the evidence bar.

The five expanded studies cover stadium design and orientation, a longer open-to-close history, rain and temperature, benign-weather overs, and real forecast vintages. Their most useful result is a sharper separation between weather affecting the game, information available before a bet, and a price that leaves enough value after the vig.

The strongest candidate is early wind-related line value

The real-forecast study’s post-hoc first-flag rule selected an under at the first usable archived vintage where ECMWF’s four-hour window mean reached 12 mph. In 2024–25 college football it selected 184 bets across 26 season-weeks, averaged +0.93 points of closing-line value, and beat its leave-one-out same-week, same-vintage baseline by +0.52 points. The nominal clustered interval for that excess is about +0.17 to +0.86 points. These are tuning-sample estimates, not a pre-registered confirmation.

The corresponding NFL sample had 72 bets, +0.89 points of raw CLV and +0.41 points over its matched baseline. The independent raw-table audit reproduced both leagues’ headline records. College’s stricter ECMWF-and-GFS variant also looked promising, but it is another tested rule, not independent proof of the primary one.

CLV is not expected profit. Under a fair-closing-price, no-push approximation, the college 0.93-point raw CLV translates to about +5.0 percentage points of incremental ROI. Subtracting the −110 vig leaves roughly +0.5% net. That conversion assumes the close is a suitable fair-value anchor; it is not a validated profit forecast. The observed college record was 101–83 with about +4.66% realized ROI at saved odds, which remains a noisy, selected historical result.

The 2026 post-hoc college check had 43 opportunities at the best standing sportsbook offer, with about +1.49 points of raw CLV and +1.31 points over its matched baseline. Those prices retain an actual book, its line, its paired odds and its quote timestamp. These games were already inspected while the policy was developed, so “2026 confirmation” would overstate their independence. The NFL check has only eight bets.

Timing is not settled by the long-history model

The separate timing study estimated a +0.64-point model-based NFL update at 72 hours for a 12 mph forecast. Its p-value of 0.023 becomes q=0.230 after accounting for all ten net-of-drift timing tests in that league. None survives. Its joint historical benchmark also failed its original negative-control validation. The earlier claims that 72 hours is the right entry time, that the opener should be avoided, or that roughly 1.2 points of NFL closing mispricing is identified are withdrawn.

The two studies answer different questions: first-flag CLV describes how a forecast-based entry compared with a later price; the conditional update model tries to identify an efficient-price gap. Their disagreement cannot be resolved by choosing the more profitable-looking headline.

Use the publication clock

Vintage 4 is not “four days before kickoff.” Complete model windows in this archive typically became usable around 131, 106, 82, 58, 34 and 10 hours before kickoff for vintages 6 through 1. At 96 hours, vintage 5 may be the freshest usable vintage. Actual publication availability, forecast fetch time and the first subsequent executable quote must determine a prospective decision.

What the broader weather evidence says

Direction and stadium effects

In college confirmation seasons, the crosswind-minus-along-field contrast was about −0.28 scoring points per mph against the close, with standard error 0.083. Development was weaker: −0.12 with p=0.21. None of the 26 registered development tests survived its FDR screen. NFL development favored crosswind, while confirmation reversed sign. The college pattern is worth a fresh test, but it cannot rescue a failed development gate after confirmation has been inspected.

Individual stadium slopes were unstable. Stadium design variables did not explain useful, repeatable differences. An initial venue heterogeneity test also over-rejected in synthetic null data; calibrated results and later-season prediction give much less support for personalized venue multipliers. A stadium’s physical wind exposure and the betting market’s remaining pricing error are separate questions.

Rain, snow and temperature

At least 2.5 mm of observed rain over the four-hour window was associated with 87–54–3 under results in the NFL and 242–177–5 in college football in the rain study’s pooled samples. Those are hindsight selections. The college rain effect fell from −4.09 points in development to −1.78 in confirmation, below the pre-registered half-effect replication threshold. NFL development was too weak to qualify. Snow changed sign across eras; cold and heat did not produce stable replicated betting rules.

The screen’s agreed 23 mm rain trigger fired zero times in 2,230 eligible forecast-games in the tested archive. It should not be presented as a calibrated rain betting threshold. Lower amounts can be displayed in forecast units while a new rule is tested; this research does not justify choosing a profitable replacement cutoff.

The rain benchmark now shares season-week resampling across overlapping historical and calibration samples and gates wind, rain and temperature at one declared information instant. Modern forecast skill and weather relationships still have to be transported into earlier eras. The resulting historical efficiency diagnostics are conditional on that assumption, not identified executable rain edges.

Why calm games going over does not create an overs strategy

Calm NFL games finished about +1.57 points above the close, but indoor games also finished about +1.28 above it. The same-week calm-outdoor minus indoor difference was only +0.25 points, with standard error 0.45. A positive average score residual can reflect the long upper tail of scoring rather than a sufficiently high over win probability.

The corrected benign-weather overs study finds little absolute number CLV and no FDR survivor in its twelve primary real-forecast comparisons: minimum q≈0.144. That is absence of demonstrated edge, not proof that the strategy loses exactly the vig. Its former “about −4.5% expected net ROI” and confidence interval were not identified and have been withdrawn. Historical simulator and transported-benchmark outputs are retained only as conditional diagnostics, with pooled precision excluded from inference.

A concrete prospective test

Use the existing first-flag hypothesis as the primary candidate. The following is a paper test, not an instruction to increase live stakes. Freeze the protocol and code hashes before collecting outcomes; use only future decision times after that freeze. Because kickoff alone does not make an opportunity new, exclude any game whose qualifying forecast or proposed entry was already inspected.

  1. Primary universe and trigger. College football, open-air with known roof state. Four hourly 10 m ECMWF wind values from kickoff through +3 hours; primary threshold 12 mph. Use the first valid public vintage in the frozen vintage-6-through-1 policy. Freeze the 15 mph and ECMWF-plus-GFS rules as secondary variants with separate multiplicity accounting.
  2. Execution. Score the first observed standing quote after the policy decision at a named sportsbook, keeping its line and odds together. Preserve a fixed-book series alongside a price-ranked sportsbook series. Freeze quote freshness, consensus-outlier rejection, close construction and missing-data rules. Exchanges require measured fees and available size before economic use.
  3. Baseline and dependence. Use other eligible games at the same decision rule and season-week, with the same execution and close conventions. Record both raw CLV and excess over that baseline. Cluster inference by season-week; repeated games and common weather systems are not independent observations.
  4. Primary decision. Evaluate at a pre-specified endpoint after at least 120 new college opportunities and enough informative weeks. Require positive baseline-adjusted CLV with a one-sided clustered p<0.05 and positive net ROI at actual prices. This is a proposed validation gate, not a power guarantee; 120 bets can still miss a modest true effect. Do not repeatedly test for promotion after each winning week.
  5. Operational checks. Review timestamp, quote and outcome completeness regularly. Log every candidate, rejected quote and no-bet decision. A 40-opportunity checkpoint is for data quality, not evidence to promote a strategy. Choose any live exposure policy separately; this research does not estimate an optimal stake.

The forecast average across ECMWF, GFS and the NWS blend can improve weather prediction. Keep that meteorological estimate separate from the exact ECMWF-only trigger being evaluated; changing the predicate creates a different strategy.

Verification, limitations and source files

The expanded masters contain NFL history from 1999 and college history from 2007. Genuine timestamped forecast-and-price evidence is concentrated in 2024–26. A longer outcomes-and-observed-weather backtest cannot manufacture the missing forecast history. Samples vary by roof, source, complete weather window, opener coverage and style eligibility.

The completion fixed the timing table’s sample mismatch and missing tests, finished stadium report assembly, recomputed rain benchmark uncertainty and feature cutoffs, and rebuilt the overs forecast diagnostics with actual temperature observation timestamps. It preserves the original plans and explicitly records post-hoc changes.
